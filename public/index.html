About The Show

The podcast where pop culture collides with politics through unfiltered commentary and sharp, quick-witted insights.

From breaking down the cultural impact of blockbuster hits to exposing the political undertones of reality TV, Poplitically Correct uncovers the deeper implications of the entertainment we consume.

Bold, thought-provoking, and always on point, this is where the pop and political worlds meet.
